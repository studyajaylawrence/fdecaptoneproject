## 1. Scope

1.1 This protocol sets ward-level isolation and hand hygiene standards. It does **not** replace
the adult sepsis pathway: for screening, time zero and the first-hour bundle, follow
**CHS-PATH-010**. Where a patient is on the sepsis pathway and also meets an isolation trigger
below, both documents apply.

## 2. Isolation triggers and timing

| Trigger | Precautions | Time to isolate |
|---|---|---|
| Suspected or confirmed *C. difficile* (three or more loose stools in 24 hours) | Contact precautions, single room with en-suite | Within **2 hours** of the trigger |
| Suspected influenza or COVID-19 | Droplet precautions, single room | Within **4 hours** of the trigger |
| Known MRSA carrier on admission | Contact precautions | Before leaving the admitting area |

2.1 If no single room is available within the time limit, the nurse in charge must escalate to
the site bed manager on extension **4720** and record the delay as an incident.

2.2 Isolation room doors display the **amber** precautions sign. A red sign is used only for
airborne precautions.

## 3. Hand hygiene

3.1 Every ward must achieve at least **95%** hand hygiene compliance in the monthly observational
audit.

3.2 A ward below 95% for two consecutive months is placed on a **weekly** audit cycle until it
reaches 95% for four consecutive weeks.

## 4. Specimens

4.1 Stool specimens for *C. difficile* testing must reach the laboratory within **2 hours** of
collection, or be refrigerated at 2-8 C for up to 24 hours.

4.2 Blood culture timing for suspected sepsis is set by CHS-PATH-010 section 2, not by this
protocol.

## 5. Scope limits

5.1 The Clinical Knowledge Assistant may explain this protocol. Decisions about an individual
patient's isolation, treatment or discharge are clinical decisions under CHS-POL-001.
