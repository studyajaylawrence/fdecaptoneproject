## 1. Escalation tiers

| Tier | Trigger | Contact | Response time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 - Routine** | Clinical question with no immediate patient risk | Unit charge nurse | Within 4 hours |
| **Tier 2 - Urgent** | Deterioration risk, abnormal result needing review, Low-confidence Assistant answer marked urgent | On-call clinician for the service, pager via operator | Within 15 minutes |
| **Tier 3 - Emergency** | Suspected sepsis, airway compromise, chest pain with instability, stroke symptoms, cardiac or respiratory arrest risk | Rapid Response Team, extension 2255 | Immediate, team at bedside within 5 minutes |

## 2. Contact routes

2.1 In hours (07:00-19:00, Monday to Friday) contact the service's on-call clinician through the
service desk on extension 4100.

2.2 Out of hours, at weekends and on public holidays, contact the hospital operator on extension
**4500**, who holds the master on-call rota.

2.3 The Rapid Response Team is reached directly on extension **2255** at all times. Staff must not
wait for a Tier 2 response before calling a Tier 3 emergency.

2.4 If a Tier 2 contact is not answered within **15 minutes**, escalate to the departmental
consultant of the week. If that contact fails within a further 15 minutes, escalate to the
on-call Chief of Service.

## 3. Critical results

3.1 Critical laboratory results must be communicated to a clinician who can act on them within
**30 minutes** of the result being verified, by direct voice contact. Electronic notification
alone does not satisfy this requirement.

3.2 Contoso Health System critical result thresholds for adults:

| Result | Critical value |
|---|---|
| Serum potassium | < 2.8 mmol/L or > 6.0 mmol/L |
| Serum sodium | < 120 mmol/L or > 158 mmol/L |
| Blood glucose | < 2.8 mmol/L (50 mg/dL) or > 27.8 mmol/L (500 mg/dL) |
| Serum lactate | > 4.0 mmol/L |
| Haemoglobin | < 70 g/L |
| Platelet count | < 20 x 10^9/L |
| INR (on anticoagulant) | > 5.0 |
| Troponin | Above the site's 99th-percentile upper reference limit with a rising pattern |

3.3 The receiving clinician must read back the result and document the communication in the record
within one hour.

## 4. Escalation from the Clinical Knowledge Assistant

4.1 Questions the Assistant defers under CHS-POL-001 section 4 are routed to the clinician review
queue and follow **Tier 1** timing unless the requester marks them urgent, in which case Tier 2
timing applies.

4.2 The Assistant must never place a Tier 3 emergency call and must never be relied on to raise
one. Staff who suspect an emergency call extension 2255 directly.

4.3 Every routed item records the requester, the question, the draft answer with citations, the
confidence label and the reason for routing.

## 5. Documentation

5.1 Every Tier 2 and Tier 3 escalation is documented in the patient record within **one hour**,
naming the person contacted, the time, and the agreed plan.

5.2 Failure to escalate against these criteria is reportable as a **Category 1 safety event**
through the incident system within 24 hours.
