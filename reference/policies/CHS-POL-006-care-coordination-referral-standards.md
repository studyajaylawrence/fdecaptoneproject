## 1. Referral priorities

| Priority | Definition | Triage | First appointment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Emergency** | Immediate risk to life, limb or sight | Same day, by direct clinician-to-clinician contact | Same day |
| **Urgent** | Condition likely to deteriorate without specialist input within weeks, or suspected cancer | Within 1 business day | Within 5 business days |
| **Routine** | Stable condition needing specialist opinion | Within 3 business days | Within 10 business days |

1.1 Emergency referrals are never sent by electronic referral alone. The referring clinician speaks
to the receiving specialist and follows CHS-POL-002.

1.2 Suspected cancer referrals are always at least **urgent** priority and are tracked on the
cancer pathway tracker.

## 2. Referral content

2.1 Every referral states the clinical question, relevant history, current medications, allergies,
and the results of investigations already performed.

2.2 Referrals missing the clinical question are returned to the referrer within **1 business day**
with a reason, and do not start the appointment clock until resubmitted.

## 3. Electronic consultations

3.1 An e-consult is offered where a specialist opinion can be given without a face-to-face visit.

3.2 Specialties respond to e-consults within **3 business days**. Cardiology, endocrinology,
nephrology and dermatology all participate.

3.3 If the e-consult response recommends a face-to-face review, the referral is converted and takes
its priority from the specialist's response.

## 4. Closing the loop

4.1 The referring clinician remains responsible for the referral until a specialist report is
received and reviewed.

4.2 Referral coordinators check for unreturned reports at **21 days** and escalate to the specialty
service manager.

4.3 Patients who do not attend are contacted within **2 business days**, and the referring clinician
is informed. A second non-attendance returns the referral to the referrer.

## 5. Transport and access needs

5.1 Transport, interpreting and accessibility needs are recorded at the time of referral, not at
booking.

5.2 Interpreter-supported appointments are given **double the standard appointment length**.

## 6. Reporting

6.1 Referral timeliness is reported monthly against the targets in section 1, with a compliance
target of **95 percent** for urgent referrals triaged within one business day.

6.2 Breaches of the emergency standard are Category 1 safety events under CHS-POL-002.

## 7. Assistant scope

7.1 The Clinical Knowledge Assistant may explain referral priorities, timeframes and required
content, with citations.

7.2 The Assistant must not assign a priority to an individual patient's referral. That is a
clinical decision under CHS-POL-001.
