## 1. Minimum necessary

1.1 Workforce members access, request and disclose only the **minimum protected health information
(PHI) necessary** for the task in hand. Treatment access by the care team is exempt from the
minimum necessary limit; all other uses are not.

1.2 Bulk extracts of patient data require Privacy Office approval and a recorded purpose.

## 2. PHI and AI systems

2.1 PHI must not be entered into any AI assistant that is not on the **Approved AI Systems
Register**. The Clinical Knowledge Assistant is registered for **policy and guideline questions
only** and is **not** approved to receive PHI.

2.2 Questions to the Clinical Knowledge Assistant must be phrased without patient identifiers. If a
question cannot be asked without identifiers, it is a clinical question and belongs with the care
team under CHS-POL-001 section 4.

2.3 Where a question containing PHI is submitted, the system must refuse to answer it, route the
item for clinician review, and record a privacy exception for the Privacy Office.

2.4 Prompts, responses and review decisions are logged. Logs are treated as PHI when they contain
patient information, and are retained for **seven years**.

## 3. The 18 identifiers

The following are treated as identifiers under the de-identification standard: names; geographic
subdivisions smaller than a state; all dates more specific than a year that relate to an individual;
telephone numbers; fax numbers; email addresses; Social Security numbers; medical record numbers;
health plan beneficiary numbers; account numbers; certificate or licence numbers; vehicle
identifiers; device identifiers and serial numbers; web URLs; IP addresses; biometric identifiers;
full-face photographs and comparable images; and any other unique identifying number,
characteristic or code.

3.1 For patients aged 90 and over, ages are recorded as "90 or older" in de-identified extracts.

## 4. Handling rules

4.1 PHI is transmitted only over Contoso-managed, encrypted channels. Personal email, personal
messaging apps and unmanaged cloud storage are prohibited.

4.2 Paper containing PHI is stored in locked areas and destroyed in confidential waste.

4.3 Screens displaying PHI are locked when unattended, and privacy filters are used in public-facing
areas.

4.4 PHI is not discussed in corridors, lifts or other public areas.

## 5. Breach response

5.1 Suspected privacy breaches are reported to the Privacy Office within **24 hours** of discovery,
through the incident system or on extension 4800.

5.2 The Privacy Office completes a risk assessment within **5 business days** and determines
notification obligations.

5.3 Retaliation against a person who reports a suspected breach in good faith is prohibited.

## 6. Training and attestation

6.1 All workforce members complete privacy training at hire and annually, and attest to this policy.

6.2 Access is reviewed quarterly; dormant accounts are disabled after **45 days** without use.
